Pocket Numbers Lite

One complete number puzzle - Pair - on the full template's engine: boosters, a coin economy, a collection, a tutorial, saved runs and a responsive mobile UI. Free edition of Pocket Numbers, with no ads anywhere.

Getting started

  1. Import the package. Everything lands under Assets/PocketNumbers.
  2. Open Games/Pair/Scenes/PairTutorial.unity and press Play.
  3. Add that scene to File > Build Profiles > Scene List before you build.

There are no ads in this package. The full edition's AdMob integration, rewarded ad UI, interstitial and banner placements and the consent flow are not here - the scripts are absent, not switched off, so the build makes no ad network calls and needs no SDK.

The game

Pair - find equal values, or pairs that total ten. Clear the supported lines, work around the gaps, and extend the board with Add when you run out of moves.

LevelsTwo authored campaign rounds plus the tutorial. Seeds and rewards are in Games/Pair/Levels/Campaign.asset.
BoostersHint, Undo, Swap, Add and Magic Wand, with charges per level and a coin shop.
EconomyCoins earned per level, spent on booster charges. Persisted in PlayerPrefs through PocketEconomy.
CollectionIllustrated collectibles that unlock as you progress.
ThemeJelly Pop, the template's default look.
SavesA run in progress is restored on the next launch, including the board, score and booster charges.

Authoring a level

Levels are data. Select Campaign.asset, add a round, and set its seed, objective text and reward - the board is generated from the seed, so the same seed always produces the same puzzle.

Retheming

The look comes from Shared/Settings/ThemeLibrary.asset and the sprite atlases under Shared/Art. Swap the atlas and the palette to reskin the whole game without touching gameplay code.

What the full edition adds

The features below are not implemented in this package: the other games have no scenes, prefabs, level assets or scripts here, and the extra themes have no atlases. There is no hidden flag that unlocks them. The controls that lead to them stay in place, so you can see the shape of the finished product; pressing one says which edition provides it.

  • Four more games - Double, Ten, Chain and Thirteen, each with its own campaign, boards and rules, sharing this same engine.
  • 24 more themes - a full atlas, palette, font and collectible set per theme, switchable at runtime.
  • Six campaign rounds per game instead of two, plus the endless mode.
  • Device haptics - the native plugin, the haptic pattern assets and the settings that drive them.
  • Rewarded ads - the AdMob integration, the dummy-ad simulator for testing, interstitials between levels, a banner placement and the consent flow.

Full edition: Pocket Numbers on the Asset Store.

PlatformsTested on Windows, WebGL and Android. The UI is authored portrait and fits itself to the safe area.

Known limits

  • Two campaign levels and the tutorial. The endless mode and the remaining rounds are full-edition content.
  • One theme. The style button explains where the others are.
  • Saves are PlayerPrefs, which is per-device and not authoritative - move to a backend for anything competitive.
  • Lite and the full edition install to the same folder and share asset GUIDs. If you upgrade later, delete Assets/PocketNumbers before importing the full edition.
