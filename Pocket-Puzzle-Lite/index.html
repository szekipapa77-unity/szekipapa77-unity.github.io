Pocket Puzzle Lite

One complete triple-match puzzle on the full collection's engine: a seven-slot tray, three boosters, a coin shop, a guided tutorial, saved runs and a responsive UI. Free edition of Pocket Puzzle Collection, with no ads anywhere.

Getting started

  1. Import the package. Everything lands under Assets/Pocket.
  2. Open Triple/Scenes/TripleTutorial.unity and press Play.
  3. Add both shipped scenes to File > Build Profiles > Scene List: TripleTutorial and TripleLevel2. The level list loads them by name.

There are no ads in this package. The full edition's AdMob integration, the rewarded ad UI, the interstitial and banner placements and the consent flow are not here - the scripts are absent, not switched off, so the build makes no ad network calls and needs no SDK.

The game

Triple - tap symbols into the seven-slot tray. Three of a kind clear together. Fill the tray and the run ends, so read the board before you tap.

LevelsThe guided tutorial plus one authored level, both as scenes with their own board prefab.
BoostersThree, with charges per level and a coin shop that sells more.
TutorialAn animated pointer that finds a real legal move on the live board rather than replaying a script.
SolverTripleSolver searches the board for a clearing sequence; it drives both the hint booster and the tutorial pointer.
SavesA run in progress is restored on the next launch - board, tray, score and charges.
ThemeJelly Pop, the collection's default look.

Retheming

A theme is one PuzzleSkin asset plus a symbol atlas and a post-processing profile. Duplicate Shared/Settings/BoldPop.asset (the Jelly Pop theme), point it at your own atlas, and assign it to the SkinController on the level prefab.

Authoring a level

A level is a scene with a board prefab. Duplicate TripleLevel2, edit the board layout, and add the scene name to the build list - LevelCatalog resolves TripleLevel<n> by convention.

What the full edition adds

The features below are not implemented in this package: the other games have no scenes, prefabs or scripts here, and the extra themes have no art. There is no hidden flag that unlocks them. The controls that lead to them stay in place, so you can see the shape of the finished product; pressing one says which edition provides it.

  • Four more games - Pairs, Merge, Link and Stretch, each with its own rules, boards and levels, sharing this same engine.
  • 24 more themes - a symbol atlas, palette and post-processing profile per theme, switchable at runtime.
  • Four levels per game instead of two.
  • The collection meta layer - unlockable companions with their own progress.
  • Device haptics - the native plugin, the haptic pattern assets and the settings that drive them.
  • Rewarded ads - the AdMob integration, interstitials, a banner placement and the consent flow.

Known limits

  • The tutorial and one level. The remaining levels are full-edition content.
  • One theme. The theme button explains where the others are.
  • Saves are PlayerPrefs, which is per-device and not authoritative.
  • Lite and the full edition install to the same folder and share asset GUIDs. If you upgrade later, delete Assets/Pocket before importing the full edition.

Third-party content
