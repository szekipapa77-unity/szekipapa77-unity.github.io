Pro UI & Data Toolkit - 5 Asset Bundle

One purchase, five complete tools. This page explains how to claim them.

This package contains no tool content, by design. Each of the five tools is its own Asset Store product, and owning the bundle entitles you to every one of them at no extra cost. You claim each from its own listing, which means you also get its own version history, release notes and updates, exactly as if you had bought it on its own.

How to claim

  1. Open a tool's link below while signed in to the Unity account you bought the bundle with.
  2. The price shows as 0.00 because you own the bundle.
  3. Add it to your assets, then import it from Window > Package Manager > My Assets.

Once per tool. After that each one updates on its own.

If a price is not 0.00

Check that you are signed in with the account that bought the bundle, and that the purchase has finished processing. If it still does not show as free, email szekipapa77@gmail.com with your invoice number.

What these five are, and are not

They are five separate tools, not one framework. They share a visual language and a support address, not an API - you can claim and use any subset, and nothing here depends on anything else here. They are the parts you end up building anyway for a data-driven app or game UI: a way to show numbers, a way to scroll a lot of rows, text that works in every language, a way to keep your assets in order, and a way to put the web inside your product.

Requirements

Each tool states its own. Across the five: Unity 6000.0 or newer, Built-in, URP and HDRP (none of them draws pipeline-specific scene content), and TextMeshPro (com.unity.ugui) for TypeFlow Pro and VeloScroll.

Third-party content

None in this package. The only third-party content across the five tools is the Amiri font (SIL Open Font License 1.1) bundled with TypeFlow Pro, with its licence file. Each tool ships its own Third-Party Notices.txt.
